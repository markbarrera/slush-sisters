The pages people spent time on. The home page usually gets the most, but what matters is whether they also read /book.

Out of everyone who visited, how many made it to the booking page, and how many actually sent a booking. This is the number that matters most.

🛬 Where people land

The first page someone sees when they arrive — the front door. This is different from the most-read list: a page can be read a lot but rarely be the first one someone sees. If most people land on the home page, marketing links should point to the page that matches the ad instead.

👆 What people click

The buttons and links people tap, grouped by where on the page they are: the navigation bar at the top, the footer at the bottom, or the main content in between. This tells you whether people find what they need from the menu, or whether the page body is doing the selling.

📖 How far people read

How far down each page people scroll before leaving. 100% means they read the whole thing. Low numbers mean they leave before the important part.

The last page someone was on before they closed the tab. If lots of people leave from /book, the form might be confusing.
