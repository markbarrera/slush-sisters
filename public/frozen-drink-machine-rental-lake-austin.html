The practical constraint on Lake Austin properties is almost always the walk. A lot of these houses have the entertaining space well below the driveway, down steps or a slope to the water. Questions people in Lake Austin ask

Is Lake Austin the same as Lake Travis?

No — they are different lakes. Lake Austin sits below Mansfield Dam and runs through west Austin past Westlake and Rob Roy. Lake Travis is the larger reservoir above the dam. We serve both, but they are separate areas for us.

Can you carry the machine down to the water?

Yes, but tell us in advance. A lot of Lake Austin properties have the entertaining area well below the driveway, and we would rather plan for the carry than turn up unprepared.

What is included in the $250?

The dual-tank machine, two flavors, delivery, setup, cups, a candy garnish on every cup, and pickup the next morning. If there is a walk down to the water, that walk is included — we do the carrying. A $50 deposit holds your date and comes off the total.

Do you supply the alcohol?

No. We bring the mix frozen and ready to pour, and there is never anything else in it when it leaves us. A grown-up adds their own tequila after we have gone, using the printed instructions. Down on the water in the sun, going heavy on it is the fastest way to end up with a cold drink instead of a frozen one.

When do you pick the machine up?

The next morning. The carry back up is ours to do, and it is easier in daylight, so we leave it with you overnight rather than hauling it out at the end of the night.
