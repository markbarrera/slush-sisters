How the grown-up side works

Worth being plain about this, because it is the question we get asked most.

The machine leaves our house with mix in it. That is all we ever put in.

If you want a margarita, you buy your own tequila and add it after we have gone. We do not buy it, we do not pour it, and we are not there for it — the girls set up in the daytime and leave. We wrote that down properly here.

A tip that saves the party. If you are adding spirits to a frozen tank, go easy. Alcohol stops things freezing, and a whole bottle in one tank means you get a cold puddle instead of a frozen drink. We hand you a card at setup with the amounts on it. If you want it strong, use the chilled side instead — there is no limit on that one.

Questions grown-ups ask

How many drinks does it make?

Both tanks hold about 70 to 80 twelve-ounce cups when full, and you can mix more during the party and top it up. For most parties one fill is plenty with some left over.

Can I put alcohol in it?

You can, after we leave. It is your party and your bottle. We just never handle it — the machine goes out with regular non-alcoholic mix and comes back the same way.

Will it freeze if I add tequila?

Up to a point. Too much alcohol and it will not freeze at all. That is exactly what the chilled setting is for — nothing has to freeze, so you can make it as strong as you like.

Do the girls come to evening events?

No. They set the machine up in the daytime, before anything starts, and go home. Their dad handles anything in the evening.

Do you do public events or festivals?

No, we turn those down. We only do private parties where one person is hosting.

What does it plug into?

An ordinary outlet. It needs about two hours to freeze down, so we arrive early — tell us your start time and we will work backwards from it.
