Our six flavors

Pick any 2 for your rental. Every single cup gets a piece of candy on it that matches the flavor. That is our thing and nobody else does it.

One tank is the kids’ tank. The machine holds two flavors at once, and unless you tell us otherwise one of them is always a drink with no alcohol in it. Everybody else who rents these fills both tanks with grown-up drinks, and the kids end up with juice boxes at a party built around a machine they cannot touch.

Worried about the dye? Fair. The thing that makes blue raspberry blue is Blue 1, and plenty of parents here read labels to avoid exactly that. We are building a version of every kids’ flavor with no artificial color in it at all — the color comes from the fruit instead, which means it looks duller than the picture in your head, and we would rather show you a photo than surprise you.
