Two tanks, and both are your call

The machine holds two flavors at once, so you can go two grown-up drinks, two with no alcohol, or one of each — whatever your party is. We are glad to make either tank alcohol-free at no extra cost, so nobody gets left out. Which is which is up to you, not us.

Questions

How many flavors do we get?

Two — that is what the machine holds, one in each tank. Pick from fifteen Classic or nine Fresh Press. The two tanks are independent, so we can do one classic flavor and one Fresh Press if you like.

Can both tanks be grown-up drinks?

Yes, if that is what you want — it is your party. We are just as glad to make either tank alcohol-free at no extra cost so the kids are not left out. Tell us which on the booking form.

What is the candy on the cup?

A piece of candy on the rim that matches the flavor — a strawberry gummy on the strawberry lemonade, a blue raspberry ring on the blue raspberry, and so on. It is on every cup, every time, at no extra cost.

Worried about the artificial dye?

Fair — the thing that makes blue raspberry blue is Blue 1, and plenty of parents here read labels to avoid exactly that. Our Fresh Press tier has no artificial color at all; the color comes from the fruit instead. That is what Fresh Press is for.

Does it do cold drinks too?

Yes. The same machine will chill a drink instead of freezing it, so it works in December as well as July. Here is how that works.
