Frozen drink machine rental in Wimberley? Talk to our friend Everest.

We do not serve Wimberley — that is about 45 minutes south of us, and we would not do a great job at that distance. But we know someone who will.

Our friend Everest is 11 years old and runs his own frozen drink machine rental called EEZ Freeze out of Wimberley, Texas. He is the reason Slush Sisters exists — we saw his business first, and it is what gave us the idea to start ours up in Lakeway. He serves Wimberley and the Hill Country area around it.

If your party is in or near Wimberley, go straight to Everest. He does the same thing we do — frozen drink machine, delivery, setup, and pickup — and he has been doing it longer than us.
