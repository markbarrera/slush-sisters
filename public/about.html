Meet the Slush Sisters

We are two sisters who started a real business because we wanted to learn how real businesses work.

We are 8 and 11 years old and we live in Lakeway, Texas. We named this company, picked the colors, chose every flavor, and show up at every event in matching shirts. Our mom runs the accounts you see us on — she posts, we are what gets posted. Dad helps with the driving. Mom makes sure we have everything packed. We handle the rest.

The candy garnish was our idea. Every single cup we serve gets a piece of candy that matches the flavor. Watermelon slushie gets a Sour Patch Watermelon on the rim. Strawberry gets a strawberry gummy. Nobody else does this. It is our thing.

Slush Sisters is a registered Texas LLC with business insurance. We take this seriously even though we are kids. Schools, HOAs, and corporate events can book us with confidence.
