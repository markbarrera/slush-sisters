Questions people in Dripping Springs ask

How much is delivery to Dripping Springs?

Dripping Springs is past our free 15-mile radius, so there is a $1/mile charge on top of the $250. Send us your address with the booking request and we will give you the exact figure before you commit.

How far ahead should we book?

Further ahead than our closer areas. Dripping Springs is a longer round trip and we make it twice, so same-week bookings are harder to fit. A couple of weeks is comfortable.

What is included in the $250?

The dual-tank machine, two flavours of your choice, delivery, setup, cups, a candy garnish for every cup, and pickup the next morning. No separate delivery fee and no cleaning fee. A $50 deposit holds your date and comes off the total.

Do you supply the alcohol?

No. We rent the machine and bring the mix, already frozen and ready to pour. If you want margaritas you add your own tequila to the tank at setup. Plenty of our rentals stay non-alcoholic for kids’ parties.

When do you collect the machine?

The next morning. You keep it for the whole event and overnight, so nobody is cleaning up or watching the clock at the end of the night.
