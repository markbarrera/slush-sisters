Common questions

What is the difference between Classic and Fresh Press?

Classic uses the same bulk mix every frozen drink company around here uses. Fresh Press is citrus squeezed the morning of your party, agave instead of corn syrup, and no artificial dye in any of it. Same machine, same delivery, same next-morning pickup.

Fresh Press is not bookable yet, and we explain exactly why on its own page — the short version is that taking sugar out of a mix can make it freeze into a block, and we are testing that on our own machine before anyone pays for it.

Why is Fresh Press $125 more when limes are cheap?

Because you are not buying limes. The ingredient difference is only about $20 to $40 a party. The rest is the morning spent squeezing, the shopping trip, mix that cannot sit in a garage for a week, and the recipe testing that had to happen first. If that is not worth it to you, book the Classic — it is a good drink and it is most of our parties.

How far in advance should I book?

At least 1-2 weeks. Weekends in summer fill up fast. Earlier is always better. Fresh Press needs five days minimum because there is a shopping trip attached.

How many drinks does the machine make?

Each tank makes about 35-40 twelve-ounce drinks. Both tanks full = 70-80 drinks. Plenty for parties of 20-50 people with refills.

Do you provide alcohol?

No. We provide the frozen mixer base. Adults add their own spirits. No liquor license needed.

Where do you deliver?

Free within 15 miles of Lakeway. That covers Bee Cave, Westlake, Steiner Ranch, and most of the Lake Travis area.

What electrical outlet do I need?

A regular household outlet. Nothing special. Just needs to be close to where you want the machine.

What about cleanup?

We handle it. We pick up the machine the next morning and clean everything ourselves.

Are you insured?

Yes. Slush Sisters LLC is a registered Texas business with liability insurance. Schools, HOAs, and corporate events can book us.
