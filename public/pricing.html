Common questions

What is the difference between Classic and Fresh Press?

Classic uses the same bulk mix every frozen drink company around here uses. Fresh Press is citrus squeezed the morning of your party, agave instead of corn syrup, and no artificial dye in any of it. Same machine, same delivery, same next-morning pickup.

Fresh Press needs five days’ notice because we squeeze it the morning of your party. The whole recipe is on its own page.

Why is Fresh Press $125 more when limes are cheap?

Because you are not buying limes. The ingredient difference is only about $20 to $40 a party. The rest is the morning spent squeezing, the shopping trip, mix that cannot sit in a garage for a week, and the recipe testing that had to happen first. If that is not worth it to you, book the Classic — it is a good drink. Most people who taste them side by side stop asking.

How far in advance should I book?

At least 1-2 weeks. Weekends in summer fill up fast. Earlier is always better. Fresh Press needs five days minimum because there is a shopping trip attached.

How many drinks does the machine make?

Each tank makes about 30 twelve-ounce drinks, so both tanks full is about 60. That covers most parties up to about 45 people. Above that we bring extra batches to refill it during the party — a refill takes about 20 minutes to freeze. We only have one machine, so for a very large event we will tell you honestly whether one is enough.

Do you provide alcohol?

No. We provide the frozen mixer base. Adults add their own spirits. No liquor license needed.

Where do you deliver?

Free within 15 miles of Lakeway. That covers Bee Cave, Westlake, Steiner Ranch, and most of the Lake Travis area.

What electrical outlet do I need?

A regular household outlet. Nothing special. Just needs to be close to where you want the machine.

What about cleanup?

We handle it. We pick up the machine the next morning and clean everything ourselves.

Are you insured?

Yes. Slush Sisters LLC is a registered Texas business with liability insurance. Schools, HOAs and community events can book us.

If your HOA or school needs a certificate or a vendor form on file, ask before you book and our dad will sort it — see the page for event organizers.

Do you do HOA pool parties and school events?

Yes, and they are some of our favorites. There is a whole page for organizers with the real numbers on it — how many cups one machine makes, when you need two, and what we need from you on the day.
