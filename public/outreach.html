Outreach Rules

  1. Never film someone's house, kids, or address without asking a grown-up first.
  2. No street names, house numbers, or school names in captions.
  3. Never say where you will be at a specific time in the future. (Don't post "We'll be at 123 Oak Lane on Saturday!")
  4. If a customer says no filming, that is a no. No asking again.
  5. Dad handles: realtors, press, any grown-up-to-grown-up conversations. Harper and Finley handle: DMs to community accounts, messages to friends' parents, anything kid-to-kid.
  6. Always use the tracking links so we know what's working: slushsisters.com/go/fb for Facebook, slushsisters.com/go/ig for Instagram, slushsisters.com/go/text for texts.
  7. One outreach per day is plenty. Don't blast everyone at once. Work the list over two weeks.

This page is just for us — it's not on the website and Google can't see it. Update it as we reach out to people and learn what works. Check our dashboard to see which channels are bringing visitors.
