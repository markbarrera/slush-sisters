What makes us different

Lots of them are fine. But nobody has all four of these at once — that's us:

  • Candy on every single cup. Nobody else does this. (One company sells salt for the rim — not the same!)
  • We serve everyone the same. Two grown-up drinks, two with no alcohol, or one of each — your call, never ours. The no-alcohol option is always free and we never push it. Some others hide a kids' option as an add-on you have to find.
  • Fresh Press: we squeeze real limes the morning of your party, with no fake coloring. Others say "good mix," but nobody actually squeezes.
  • It's really run by two sisters — Harper (11) and Finley (8). No big company can copy that.

Nice ideas we could borrow (the fair way)

  • Ask for reviews after every party. River City has a huge wall of happy reviews and it makes them look great. We're new, so let's start collecting ours from party one.
  • Say exactly what you get for $250 — like "the machine, two flavors, cups, candy, delivery, and pickup." Woolf's lists it out and it builds trust.
  • Make booking actually work online. Several of them let you pick a date and pay right there. (That one's a grown-up job for Dad.)
