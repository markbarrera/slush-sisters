The part most people do not know

The machine does not only make frozen drinks. It will chill instead of freeze — so it works at a December party in a warm office as well as it does by a pool in July.

That matters for two reasons. Chilled means you are not handing people a slushie in winter. And chilled removes the ceiling that frozen has: a frozen drink stops freezing if there is too much alcohol in it, and a chilled one does not care.

Nobody else renting these advertises it. The full explanation is on the grown-ups page.

Cold-weather ideas that actually work in it

Frozen: margarita, piña colada, a frozen mulled-cider style thing if you want to be seasonal about it. Chilled: sangria, a punch, spiked cider, a cranberry cooler, or something with no alcohol in it at all so the people driving have a real option.

Carbonated is the one thing it cannot do — dispense the base, top the glass with the fizz.

Questions

Do you serve the drinks?

No, and it is better this way. It is self-serve, so there is no line and nobody is stuck behind a table all night. We show whoever is hosting how it works before we leave.

Can we do one alcoholic and one not?

Yes, and for a work party that is usually the right call. Two tanks, two flavors, and the people driving home get something that is not a soda.

Where does it need to go?

A normal outlet within about 25 feet, a flat spot, and somewhere we can wheel a cart to. If you are on an upper floor, tell us — we will need to know about the elevator and the loading situation.

Do you come to downtown Austin?

Yes. Free delivery within 15 miles of Lakeway, then $1 a mile beyond that, and we will tell you the exact number before you book. Our Austin page is honest about how far the van goes.

How late can we keep it?

Overnight. We collect it the next morning, so nothing has to be packed up while people are still there.

Can we get the premium recipe for a work party?

Yes — that is Fresh Press at $375, made with fresh limes and agave rather than mix. It needs five days’ notice because we squeeze it the morning of. For a client-facing event it is the one to book.

Can we put our company logo on the cart?

Yes — that is our branded setup add-on. We put your logo on a banner at the cart and on the flavor cards at the machine. If you have custom cup sleeves or stickers, we use those too. Email us what you are planning and we will give you a price.

Tell us your date

Headcount, the date, and whether you want it frozen or chilled. That is enough for us to come back with a straight answer the same day.
