Launch Plan

The step-by-step plan for going public with Slush Sisters. Work through each phase in order. Check things off as you go — your progress saves on this device.

The goal: Get our first 10 real bookings. Not followers, not likes — bookings. Everything on this list is chosen because it gets us closer to someone typing their address into the booking form.

🛠️ Phase 0 — Make Sure It All Works

Before anything else

Don't tell anyone about us until the basics work. A visitor who finds a broken site won't come back.

  • Booking form sends real emails when someone fills it out Dad
    Test it yourself — fill out the form and check that the email arrives in the inbox.
  • Site loads fast on a phone Harper or Finley
    Open slushsisters.com on your phone. Does it load in under 3 seconds? Can you find the Book button without scrolling?
  • Every page has correct info — price ($250), service area, flavors Harper or Finley
    Read through every page on your phone. Flag anything that looks wrong or old.
  • Google Business Profile created Dad
    Go to business.google.com and set up "Slush Sisters" with the Lakeway address. This is how we show up when someone googles "frozen drink rental near me."
  • Instagram handle secured Dad
    @slushsisters is taken. Try @slushsistersatx, @slushsisterstx, or @theslushsisters. Claim it and put slushsisters.com/go/ig in the bio.

📣 Phase 1 — Tell the Inner Circle

Week 1

Start with people who already know you. They'll be your first customers and your first word-of-mouth.

  • Text 10 friends' parents Harper and Finley
    "Hey! We started a frozen drink machine rental business. We deliver to your house, set it up, and pick it up — $250 for the whole day. Check it out: slushsisters.com/go/text"
  • Tell your teachers and school friends Harper and Finley
    Just tell them in person. "We started a business!" People will ask to see the website.
  • Post the first Instagram story Harper or Finley
    A 15-second video of the machine running with candy on a cup. Caption: "We're open! 🍧" with a link to slushsisters.com/go/ig
  • Dad texts his parent friends Dad
    Same message, to the grown-up network. Use slushsisters.com/go/text so the dashboard counts it.

🏘️ Phase 2 — Hit the Neighborhood Groups

Week 1-2

This is where bookings come from. A mom in a Facebook group asking "does anyone know someone who does frozen drinks?" is the single best customer we can find. We want to be the answer before anyone asks.

  • Post in "Lakeway, Bee Cave, Lake Travis area — News & Chatter" Dad
    Short, honest post. Include slushsisters.com/go/fb. Don't be salesy — just say what you do, the price, and that the girls run it.
  • Post in Steiner Ranch groups Dad
    Same message, different group. One post per group, not a blast.
  • Post in Rough Hollow / Belterra groups Dad
    Same approach. These are the neighborhoods in our delivery zone.
  • Post on Nextdoor Dad
    Use slushsisters.com/go/nd for the link. Nextdoor lets you post to nearby neighborhoods.
  • Check the dashboard — are clicks coming in? Anyone
    Look at the "Tracking Links" section. Which source is getting the most clicks?

🤝 Phase 3 — Outreach

Week 2-3

Now reach people who don't know us yet. Work through the outreach list — one message per day, no blasting.

  • Message @lakewaymoms on Instagram Harper
    See the outreach list for the suggested message. Offer a giveaway.
  • Email Lake Travis Lifestyle / Cathy Hale Dad
    Pitch the kid-entrepreneur story. See outreach list for the angle.
  • Contact 2 bounce house companies for cross-referral Dad
    We Bring The Party, iParty and Bounce, or Sky High. "We do drinks, you do bounce houses — same party. Let's refer each other."
  • Offer one free setup to a local organization Dad
    Little League, school PTA, fire station. Cost: one batch of mix. Return: their entire parent network hears about us.
  • Pitch one Lakeway realtor for an open house setup Dad
    Start with one agent you know. Offer the first one free. See outreach list.

📰 Phase 4 — Press

Week 3-4

Once you have a few bookings (or at least one great setup photo), pitch the local news. The story writes itself: two kids, a real LLC, a real business.

  • Email Community Impact — Lake Travis / Westlake edition Dad
    Short pitch: "Two Lakeway sisters started a frozen drink rental business." Include a photo and the website.
  • Email KVUE or KXAN features desk Dad
    Subject: "Two sisters (11 and 8) started a frozen drink rental business in Lakeway." Offer a setup they can film.
  • Email Lake Travis View Dad
    Same pitch, local paper.
  • Submit to Austin Moms for a feature or giveaway Dad
    Pitch a profile or a giveaway collab. See outreach list.

🔄 Phase 5 — Keep Going

Ongoing

After the launch push, the work shifts to two things: posting content and following up. Half your posts should have nothing to do with slushies — just be yourselves.

  • Post 2-3 times per week on Instagram Harper and Finley
    Mix it up: one about the business, one just being sisters, one about something trending. Don't let every post be "book us!"
  • Reply to every Facebook group question about party rentals Dad
    When someone asks "does anyone know a frozen drink rental?" — that's your moment. Be helpful, not salesy.
  • Check the dashboard weekly Anyone
    Which tracking links are getting clicks? Which pages do people read? Is anyone making it to the booking page?
  • After every booking: ask for a photo you can share Harper or Finley
    "Can we share a photo from your party?" Most people say yes. One real party photo is worth ten staged ones.
  • After 10 bookings: review what worked Everyone
    Look at the dashboard. Where did those 10 customers come from? Do more of whatever that was.
