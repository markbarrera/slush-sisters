How it works

You book

Pick your date and choose two flavors. A $50 deposit holds it.

We deliver

We arrive before your party, set the machine up, mix the first batch, and show everyone how to pour.

You party

Guests pour all day and night. We come back the next morning and take the machine away.

One thing worth knowing: Slush Sisters is run by two sisters, ages 8 and 11, with their dad driving the van. They pick the flavors, match the candy to each one, and handle the setup. It is a real business and they run it — which is either a fun detail or the whole reason you book us.

Questions people ask

How much is it?

A rental is $250. That covers the machine, two flavors, delivery, setup, cups, and the candy garnish. There is no separate delivery fee and no cleaning fee. A $50 deposit holds your date and comes off the total.

Do you supply the alcohol?

No. We are a machine rental — we bring the machine and the mix, already frozen and ready to pour. If you want margaritas, you add your own tequila to the tank. Plenty of our rentals stay non-alcoholic and go to kids’ birthday parties instead.

How many flavors do we get?

Two. The machine has two tanks, so you pick two from the flavor list and they both run all day. Watermelon, Strawberry Lemonade, Blue Raspberry, Mango, Margarita Lime, and Piña Colada.

When do you pick the machine up?

The next morning. You keep it for the whole event and overnight, so nobody has to watch the clock or clean anything up at the end of the night.

Where do you deliver?

Lakeway, Bee Cave, and around Lake Travis, plus the nearby west Austin neighborhoods. If you are not sure whether you are in range, ask — we would rather tell you than have you guess.

What is the candy garnish?

Every cup comes with a candy that matches its flavor. Sour Patch Watermelon with the watermelon, a strawberry gummy with the strawberry lemonade, and so on. It is the part people photograph.
