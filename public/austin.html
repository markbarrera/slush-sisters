Questions people in Austin ask

How far will you actually come?

Free inside our home area — Lakeway, Bee Cave, Lake Travis and the neighborhoods right around them. Beyond that we will usually still come, but it depends on the day and what else we have on. Ask us and we will tell you honestly rather than making you guess.

Are you really two kids?

Yes. Finley is 10 and Harper is 8. We do the mixing, the flavors, the setup, the garnish and the messages. Our dad drives, because we cannot.

Can we put alcohol in it?

The machine leaves our house with regular non-alcoholic mix in it and that is all we ever put in. What grown-ups add after we have gone is their business, not ours. We do not buy it, we do not pour it, and we are not there for it.

How many drinks does it make?

Both tanks hold about 70 to 80 twelve-ounce cups when full, and you can mix more during the party and top it up. For most birthday parties one fill is plenty and there is some left over.

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can, especially May through August, which is our busiest stretch. We have one machine, so once a Saturday is taken it is taken. Winter is much easier to get.

Do you do anything other than birthday parties?

Yes — pool parties, block parties, school events, teacher appreciation days, open houses and work parties. If you are not sure whether it fits, just ask.
