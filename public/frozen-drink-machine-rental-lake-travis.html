Lake house parties, and the things worth knowing

Two practical things come up on nearly every lake booking. First, power: a lot of lake houses have their entertaining space on a lower deck or by the water, away from the outlets. The machine needs a standard outlet it can stay plugged into for the whole party, so it is worth checking what is reachable before we arrive rather than discovering it during setup.

Second, sun. Decks over the water get full afternoon sun with nowhere to hide, and a machine in direct heat produces a softer drink. If there is a shaded corner or a spot under an overhang, that is where it should go.

Because Lake Travis covers a wide area, some addresses are inside our free 15-mile radius and some are not. Spicewood and Briarcliff sit toward the outer edge. Send us the address with your booking and we will tell you the exact number before you commit — there is a $1/mile charge past 15 miles and we would rather you knew up front.

This is also the area where we get the most adult bookings. We rent the machine and bring the mix; you add your own tequila. We do not sell or serve alcohol.

Questions people in Lake Travis ask

Do you deliver to Spicewood?

Yes, though Spicewood is near the edge of our free radius, so there may be a $1/mile charge past 15 miles. Send us the address and we will give you the exact figure before you book.

Can you set up on a dock or a lower deck?

As long as there is a standard outlet the machine can stay plugged into, and a flat surface. We will not run a machine anywhere it could get knocked into the water, so a deck set back from the edge is better than the end of a dock.

Is it better for a bachelorette or a birthday?

Both work. The machine runs two flavours at once, so a lake house party can have margaritas in one tank and something non-alcoholic in the other — which is the usual setup when there are kids around as well.

What is included in the $250?

The dual-tank machine, two flavours of your choice, delivery, setup, cups, a candy garnish for every cup, and pickup the next morning. No separate delivery fee and no cleaning fee. A $50 deposit holds your date and comes off the total.

Do you supply the alcohol?

No. We rent the machine and bring the mix, already frozen and ready to pour. If you want margaritas you add your own tequila to the tank at setup. Plenty of our rentals stay non-alcoholic for kids’ parties.

When do you collect the machine?

The next morning. You keep it for the whole event and overnight, so nobody is cleaning up or watching the clock at the end of the night.
