## Start here, in this order

   Free weekly newsletter, 4,000+ local readers, with a feature slot built for

   inside the service area. Best phone call available.

   3423 Bee Caves Rd, West Lake Hills — about four miles away.
   **Sat 1 May 2027, applications open now**, 36 booths, host Jayme Bynum.
   The kids must do setup and selling themselves, which is the point.

   Prints the monthly newsletter for Bee Cave, Lakeway, Belterra, Steiner Ranch,
   Spicewood and Briarcliff. Advertiser-funded. Steiner Ranch's *Ranch Record*
   alone goes to 5,000 homes.

   Its charter names Lakeway, Bee Cave, Westlake and Spicewood.

> **Operational constraint found during research:** most kid-vendor markets
> restrict food sales — the Austin Kids Market listing said "prepackaged food
> and baked goods only." A machine dispensing drinks on site is a prepared-food
> operation needing a Travis County or City temporary food permit, and the
> margarita side adds TABC exposure. At kid markets they will more likely be
> allowed to *market* the rental business than to *sell drinks*. Confirm with
> each host before applying.

## Platform rules — read before scaling any account

Both girls are under 13, so every account must be **created, owned and operated
by Mark, in his own name**. Not a workaround — it is the compliant setup and
each platform names this case.

- **Meta (July 2025):** adult-run accounts that primarily feature children get strict message settings and Hidden Words on by default, are not recommended to suspicious adults, and **cannot offer subscriptions or receive gifts**. Meta removed ~135,000 Instagram accounts leaving sexualised comments on such accounts, plus ~500,000 linked accounts. Keep the strict defaults on.
- **TikTok:** parents may post content featuring their children, but "it needs to be clear that the account belongs to an adult" — say so in the bio. Content from accounts registered as under-16 is **not eligible for For You recommendation**.
- **YouTube:** AdSense is 18+ and pays the guardian. Live streaming requires 16+; ages 13–15 need an adult visibly on camera. The "Made for Kids" designation is a COPPA legal obligation with real revenue consequences — worth an attorney's read before the channel scales.
- **Child creator earnings laws** exist in Illinois, Minnesota, California, Utah and Arkansas. **Texas has none.** That is a legal floor, not a recommendation — those states legislated because the absence of a rule produced bad outcomes.

The honest framing: these accounts will build audience, credibility and
bookings. They will not produce meaningful ad revenue for years.
