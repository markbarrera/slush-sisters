# Leander Party Rentals — snapshot 2026-08-05

- **Site:** https://leanderpartyrentals.com  (WordPress + InflatableOffice
  rental software). A **general one-stop party-rental company**, not a margarita
  specialist. Serves Austin, Cedar Park, surrounding.
- **Phone:** 512-528-3099 · info@leanderpartyrentals.com

**Catalog (breadth is the point):** tables & chairs, inflatables/bounce houses,
tents, **concessions**, staging, heating & cooling, lighting, games, party
add-ons. **Real online booking** — date picker, live availability, cart,
delivery-address check, checkout.

**Concessions pricing:** 12oz popcorn $75 · snow cone $60 · cotton candy $60–70 ·
**Frozen Drink / Margarita Machine, Double Bowl $175** · YETI cooler $15. (All
pricing per 24-hour period.)

**Their margarita machine is a commodity afterthought** — cheapest in the set at
$175, no flavors listed, no mix/candy/service, just the box among many rentals.

**Takeaways:** (1) **real booking with live date availability is table stakes** —
they, Woolf's (Square), and Hill Country all have it; our form still fakes
success (`site-audit.md`). Biggest gap. (2) A general party-rental firm is a
**referral channel**, not just a rival — they'd rather refer a margarita
specialist than stock flavors/candy/mix. Worth an outreach relationship. (3) The
$175 bare box is the **price floor/anchor** — our $250 must visibly out-include
it (flavors, candy, delivery, service, the kids' tank). (4) Do **not** copy the
breadth — sprawling into a general catalog would dissolve our focus, which is the
moat.
