**Takeaways:** (1) the "kids slushee package" and "with or without alcohol" are
NOT novel — this relic already sells them. Our differentiation is framing/default
+ candy + fresh-squeezed + founders, not the mere existence of a kids option.
(2) "Fresh cut limes $20" shows there's demand for fresh citrus — validates Fresh
Press. (3) A promise page in this category is expected — ours is stronger.
(4) Their site is the cautionary design tale: great-enough offer, but the site
looks untrustworthy and has no mobile/booking. Our biggest free win is looking
legit where they don't.
