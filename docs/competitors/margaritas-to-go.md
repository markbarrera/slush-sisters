**This is the strongest competitor.** Why:

- **Positioning:** "Austin's largest and most experienced provider of frozen
  drink machine rentals." Franchise muscle + award-winning mix.
- **Pricing:** $149–$349 (varies by machine/season/location). First gourmet mix
  free. Extra mix $25 (refundable if unopened).
- **Included:** free delivery/setup/pickup (local), first mix free, 5-gal mixing
  container, **50 cups, 50 straws**, margarita salt rimmer, cart with skirting.
- **Ingredients claim (overlaps our Fresh Press):** "All of our mixes are made
  with **pure cane sugar**… A vast majority of margarita mix on the market is
  full of **high-fructose corn syrup and artificial flavors… but not ours.**"
  Several flavors "made with organic flavorings." — This is the same corn-syrup/
  no-artificial argument our Fresh Press makes. BUT it is still **bulk Margarita
  Man concentrate**, not fresh-squeezed the morning of. Fresh Press (real limes,
  same-day, no dye at all) still beats it — but the "no corn syrup / organic"
  high ground is **contested**, not ours alone. Say "fresh-squeezed" and "no
  artificial dye," not just "no corn syrup."
- **Kids / non-alcohol:** explicit — mixes "perfect for adult beverages **as
  well as slushies for kids' parties**." Many kid-coded flavors: Blue Raspberry
  ("bubblegum taste… popular kid flavor"), Cherry Bomb ("kids love it"),
  Dreamsicle ("great for kids"), Strawberry Banana ("kid favorite"). ~26 flavors
  incl. Neutral Base (make-your-own), Frosé, Hurricane, White Russian, Mudslide.
- **Trust:** dedicated testimonials page; "we even get business from our
  competitors"; partners with bars/restaurants (Backyard Bar & Grill, Dos
  Mary's, Cisco's, Olive or Twist, Drink Slingers, Austin Bounce House Rentals);
  sells wholesale mix to bars.
- **Alcohol model:** customer supplies all liquor (same as us).

**Takeaways for us:** (1) the "no corn syrup / kids too" ground is already taken
here — our edge must be **fresh-squeezed same-day + zero artificial dye + candy
garnish + two real kid founders**, not just "no corn syrup." (2) They lean on
franchise scale + social + testimonials + partner network — all things we can't
match on scale but can beat on *story* and *design*. (3) Their partner/wholesale
model is a channel idea. (4) Design is a dated rental-platform template — we win
on design and voice.
