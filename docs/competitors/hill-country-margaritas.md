# Hill Country Margaritas ("Hill Country Margs") — snapshot 2026-08-05

- **Site:** https://hillcountrymargaritas.com  (modern React/Vite build — the
  design benchmark of the set)
- **Pages:** home, event-type (flavor-picker booking flow), book, request-quote,
  about, privacy.

**Design (why it's "great looking"):** full-bleed **hero photo of the actual
machine set up poolside in the Hill Country at golden hour**; one headline
("Unlimited Margaritas. Legendary Parties."); a single amber CTA ("Reserve
Margarita Machine"); a **3-icon benefit row** (commercial-grade · unlimited
refills · delivery & pickup); **flavor cards with appetizing drink photos**;
tight FAQ; minimal 3-item nav. Aspirational, premium, adult.

**Flavors (3):** Classic Margarita, Strawberry Daiquiri, Piña Colada.
**Model:** "unlimited flavor refills," commercial-grade, delivery/pickup incl.
Booking = pick flavor → event-type flow → quote. Customer supplies alcohol
(their FAQ: "Do you supply the alcohol? How much do I use?").

**Takeaways for us:** steal the **structure** — one promise, one CTA, an icon
"what's included" row, appetizing product cards, a short FAQ, a real booking
flow. Do NOT steal the **glossy adult-cocktail aesthetic** (it's exactly the
alcohol-brand look our visual-identity doc rejects, and it has no kids/no
character). Their weakness: only 3 flavors, no kids angle, no story, generic
premium. We can look just as polished while being unmistakably *ours*.
