# Content ideas — for Finley and Harper

This is your idea list. When you want to post something and cannot think what,
open this, pick one, and go do it. Cross things off. Add your own at the bottom —
your ideas are usually better than ideas someone thought up at a desk.

You do not have to do these in order. You do not have to do all of them. Two
good posts a week beats seven boring ones.

---

## The one big secret

Here is the thing most people get wrong, so you get a head start.

**People do not share videos about slushies. They share videos about two kids
running a real business.**

A video of a nice drink gets a like. A video of you counting the money you made
and working out how much you still owe Dad for the machine gets *sent to
someone*. That is the whole difference, and it is worth remembering every time
you pick what to film.

So when you are stuck: show the business, not just the drink.

---

## Rules

Short list, and these ones actually matter.

1. **Never film someone's house, their kids, or their address** without asking a
   grown-up in that family first. Some people care a lot about this. Asking
   first costs nothing; getting it wrong loses a customer forever.
2. **No street names, no house numbers, no school name in a caption.**
3. **Never say where you are going to be at a specific time in the future.**
   Afterwards is fine.
4. If a customer says no filming, that is a no. Do not ask twice.
5. If something feels weird, stop and ask Mom or Dad. You will never be in
   trouble for stopping.

---

## What makes a post good

- **Show the thing, do not describe it.** Filming the machine pouring beats
  saying "our machine pours really well."
- **The first two seconds decide everything.** Start with the most interesting
  moment, not with "hi guys."
- **Real beats perfect.** A messy spill you laugh about does better than a
  perfect shot. People can tell the difference and they like the real one.
- **One idea per video.** Do not try to say five things.
- **Talk like yourselves.** You are 10 and 8 and that is the entire appeal. Do
  not try to sound like an adult ad.

## The idea bank

### Money and business — post these most

These are your best ones. Nobody else can make them.

1. Count the cash box after a party. Say the real number.
2. Update how much you still owe Dad for the machine. Do this every month.
3. Work out the profit on one party out loud — what came in, what it cost, what
   is left.
4. Show what you actually spend money on: cups, mix, candy, straws.
5. "We made our first $1,000" — whenever it happens, film that.
6. Explain why a rental costs $250 and what is included.
7. Do the maths on a Dripping Springs booking with the mileage charge, and
   decide out loud whether it is worth it.
8. Show the LLC paperwork. Explain what an LLC even is.
9. Open the business mail.
10. Talk about what you are saving for.
11. "Should we buy a second machine?" — argue both sides.
12. What you would do differently if you started again.

### The drinks

13. Rank all six flavours worst to best. Disagree with each other.
14. Blind taste test — can you tell which flavour is which?
15. Show all six candy garnishes lined up with their matching flavour.
16. Invent a brand new flavour and try it.
17. Invent a flavour that turns out terrible. Post it anyway. This one always
    does well.
18. Mix a whole batch from scratch, sped up.
19. Time-lapse of slush actually freezing in the tank.
20. Pour the very first cup of the day.
21. Layer two flavours in one cup and see what happens.
22. What the inside of the machine looks like.
23. Clean the machine. Weirdly, people love watching this.
24. Try every flavour with Tajín and rate them.
